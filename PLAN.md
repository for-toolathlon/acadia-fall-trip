## Park overview
Acadia National Park is the national park in Maine. It is the only national park on the Atlantic coast and the oldest national park east of the Mississippi River. The park comprises roughly 47,000 acres along the coast and around Mount Desert Island, plus nearby islands (most notably Mount Desert and Isle au Haut). Key features include Cadillac Mountain (highest point on the US Atlantic coast), the Park Loop Road (27 miles), carriage roads, coastal hiking trails, and the carriage roads system.
