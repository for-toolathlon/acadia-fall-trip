# acadia-fall-trip — Planning Home Base

## Park overview
- **Park:** Acadia National Park — Maine
- **Designation:** National Park
- **Park code:** `acad` (four-letter code: acad)
- **Description:** Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads.
- **Location:** Mount Desert Island, Maine (Schoodic Peninsula and Isle au Haut)
- **Activities:** Hiking, biking, boating, camping, stargazing, photography, etc.

## Campgrounds
1. **Blackwoods Campground** — Otter Creek, ME (east side of Mount Desert Island, ~5 mi south of Bar Harbor on Route 3). Amenities: flush toilets (seasonal), running water, dump station, picnic tables and fire rings; paved roads; no electrical hookups. Sites: 281 (tent-only 221, RV non-electric 60). Season: early May to mid-October (reservation via Recreation.gov, 90% released 6 months in advance).
2. **Seawall Campground** — Southwest Harbor, ME (west side of Mount Desert Island). Amenities: flush toilets (seasonal), running water, dump station, camp store, amphitheater; no electrical hookups. Sites: 202 (tent-only 43, RV non-electric 59, walk-in 98). Season: Wednesday before Memorial Day to Columbus Day.
3. **Schoodic Woods Campground** — Winter Harbor, ME (Schoodic Peninsula, ~1.5 mi SE of Winter Harbor). Amenities: flush/vault toilets, running water, potable water, dump station, amphitheater, camp store; 78 sites with electrical hookups; chairs with electrical hookups. Sites: 89. Season: Wednesday before Memorial Day Weekend until Columbus Day.
4. **Duck Harbor Campground** — Isle au Haut (Bar Harbor, ME mailing address). Amenities: composting toilets (seasonal), trash/recycling, potable water, dump station; no electrical hookups. Sites: 5 (lean-to shelters, walk/boat-to). Season: reservations required only (released 10 am April 1).
